# 2000CAR podcast brand and cast specification

Read this reference whenever selecting people, reframing footage, or building the recurring graphic package.

## Cast priority

1. **兩千 — protagonist.** Brown shirt in the established EP.150 setup, no glasses. He owns the opening, important reactions, visual continuity, and preferably the final beat.
2. **阿嘎 — supporting role.** Black shirt and glasses. Cut to him for a necessary answer, correction, disagreement, or punchline, then return to 兩千.
3. **阿宏 — off-camera, lowest priority.** Keep his voice only when the question or short response is necessary to understand the exchange. Remove redundant setup, repeated wording, and long explanations. Do not let an off-camera line dominate a 13-second clip.

Target 兩千 at roughly 65–80% of visible runtime. A stronger bias is acceptable when 阿宏 supplies only a very short prompt. Reaction shots of 兩千 may play over another person's audio when they are naturally listening; do not create misleading lip-sync.

## Framing

- Delivery canvas: 1080×1920 vertical, 60 fps for the established format.
- Crop each visible person to a centered medium close-up. Keep the face and microphone balanced around the vertical axis.
- Use hard cuts on conversational handoffs. Avoid decorative camera motion unless explicitly requested.
- For the known 1920×1080 EP.150 setup, the proven 432×768 crop origins are approximately `x=274` for 兩千 and `x=1228` for 阿嘎 before scaling to 1080×1920. Re-measure on other episodes.

## Graphic package

- Upper-left show mark: fluorescent yellow with black text.
- Main hook: fluorescent-yellow rounded pill in the lower-middle area, black heavy text, centered.
- Use **Noto Sans TC weight 900** for hook headlines, with about 5 px tracking and automatic sizing around 54–72 px. Maintain generous side margins.
- Do not use Mantou Sans／饅頭黑體 for these long single-line headlines unless a full-resolution and phone-scale QA proves every glyph remains open. It previously collapsed into unreadable black shapes.
- Transcript captions: approximately 84 px, white heavy text on a translucent black box, with one short keyword or number highlighted fluorescent yellow.
- Keep captions above the yellow headline and ensure overlays never cover them.
- Bottom lockup: episode label plus the long-episode title, white heavy type with a subtle dark shadow.

## Audio

- Preserve clear dialogue as the primary signal.
- Artlist music may be used when the user's commercial license/session is available.
- Established EP.150 bed: Artlist “Jinx - Short version a”. A proven starting gain is `0.035`, with short edge fades.
- No automatic ducking or voice-triggered volume automation. Adjust only the constant music gain if it competes with speech.
- Aim near -14 LUFS integrated and keep true peak around -1.5 dBFS or lower; prioritize intelligible speech over exact numeric matching.
